**Version 0.0.1 | Effective from 1 November 2026 / प्रभावी दिनांक: १ नोव्हेंबर २०२६**

### In Short / थोडक्यात
- PhotoOS acts as a **Data Fiduciary** under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) for the platform services we deliver directly to you. Photographers and Studios act as independent Data Fiduciaries for data they independently collect during shoots.
- We collect account details, booking metadata, and event photos. We **never** store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI PINs; these are processed securely by RBI-licensed payment gateways.
- **Facial Recognition / Vector Data is 100% Opt-In**: Face-matching is an optional convenience tool to help event guests find their photos. Face vectors (mathematical embeddings) are stored temporarily, never sold, never used for behavioral advertising or public training, and can be deleted at any time with a single click.
- You have the statutory right under the DPDP Act 2023 to access, correct, erase your personal data, nominate a representative, or lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer or the Data Protection Board of India.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect
We collect only such digital personal data as is necessary, lawful, and directly relevant to providing PhotoOS services:

2.1. **Account and Profile Data:** Full name, mobile phone number, email address, photography studio brand name, physical business address, city, and tax identifiers (GSTIN/PAN where applicable for invoicing).

2.2. **Client Booking & Event Metadata:** Event date, event location/city, booking category (e.g., Wedding, Corporate, Portrait), milestone deliverables, and client contact information.

We do **not** store raw facial biometric scan images; we store only mathematical arrays for search comparison.

2.5. **Technical & Session Metadata:** IP address (hashed for privacy), device hardware parameters, operating system, browser type, referral links, login timestamps, and access tokens.

2.6. **Payment References:** We store only transaction identifiers, order IDs, invoice numbers, and payment status returned by our payment aggregator. **We never collect or store debit/credit card numbers, CVVs, expiry dates, or UPI PINs.**

## 4. Special Notice on Facial Recognition & Biometric Vector Data
4.1. **Explicit, Separate Opt-In:** Facial recognition is **never mandatory** to view or download event galleries. It is presented as a separate, unticked, optional toggle.

4.2. **What Is Stored:** We generate mathematical numeric vectors (embeddings consisting of 128 or 512 floating-point values) that represent relative facial geometry. No raw face templates are shared with government databases or commercial ad brokers.

4.3. **Purpose & Access Limitation:**
- Face vectors are utilized solely to filter photos within that specific event or studio gallery.
- Only the individual performing the search and authorized studio managers can view the matched results.
- Face vectors are **never sold, leased, rented, or used for behavioral profiling, credit scoring, or targeted advertising**.
- Face vectors are **never used to train open-source or public foundation AI models**.

4.4. **Right to Withdraw & Purge:** Event guests can withdraw their facial search consent at any time via the self-service privacy portal or by emailing [privacy@photoos.in] [CONFIRM WITH LAWYER]. Upon withdrawal, the individual's face vectors are permanently purged from our database within twenty-four (24) hours.

## 5. Rights of Data Principals (Under the DPDP Act, 2023)
As a Data Principal in India, you are endowed with non-waivable statutory rights under Chapter III of the DPDP Act 2023:

5.1. **Right to Access Information (Section 11):** You have the right to obtain a summary of your personal data being processed, the identities of all Data Processors with whom it has been shared, and related processing information.

5.2. **Right to Correction and Erasure (Section 12):** You have the right to correct inaccurate or misleading personal data, complete incomplete records, update outdated information, and request the permanent erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

5.3. **Right to Grievance Redressal (Section 13):** You have the right to register a grievance with our designated Grievance Officer regarding any act or omission of PhotoOS related to your personal data.

5.4. **Right to Nominate (Section 14):** You have the statutory right to nominate any individual who, in the event of your death or incapacity, shall exercise your rights as a Data Principal.

5.5. **Exercise of Rights & Timelines:** You may exercise any of these rights directly through the "Privacy & Data Rights" portal in your PhotoOS account settings, or by submitting a written request to [privacy@photoos.in] [CONFIRM WITH LAWYER]. We will respond to and resolve all verified statutory requests within thirty (30) days [CONFIRM WITH LAWYER].

## 6. Protection of Children’s Personal Data (Under 18 Years)
6.1. **Verifiable Parental Consent (Section 9):** PhotoOS does not knowingly register accounts for children under eighteen (18) years of age without verifiable consent from their parent or lawful guardian.

6.2. **Prohibition of Tracking and Behavioral Profiling:** We strictly do not engage in tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising directed at individuals who are identified as minors.

6.3. **Event Photographs Containing Children:** Photographs of minors taken during family, school, or wedding events and uploaded by studios must have been captured with the express consent of the child's parent or legal guardian. If a parent requests the takedown of their child's photograph from any gallery, PhotoOS will honor and execute such takedown immediately upon verification.

## 8. Technical and Organizational Security Safeguards
PhotoOS deploys robust technological and physical safeguards conforming to Section 8(5) of the DPDP Act and ISO/IEC 27001 standards:
- **Encryption in Transit & At Rest:** All data exchanged is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption. Stored assets on Cloudflare R2 and PostgreSQL tables on Supabase are encrypted using AES-256.
- **Row-Level Security (RLS):** Supabase database tables enforce strict database-level isolation ensuring that one studio cannot read another studio's client data.
- **Signed Storage URLs:** Client photographs stored on Cloudflare R2 are accessible only via short-lived, cryptographically signed pre-authenticated URLs.
- **Access Control & Audit Trails:** Administrative access to production databases is restricted to authorized personnel via multi-factor authentication (MFA) and logged for continuous audit.
