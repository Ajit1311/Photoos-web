# PhotoOS Tripartite Platform Agreement

**Version 0.0.1 | Effective from 1 November 2026 / प्रभावी दिनांक: १ नोव्हेंबर २०२६**

### In Short / थोडक्यात
- **Intermediary Status**: PhotoOS is solely a technology platform and marketplace intermediary within the meaning of Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020.
- **Independent Direct Service Contract**: The contract for photography services, event shoots, and creative deliverables is entered into **directly and exclusively between the Client and the Photographer**. PhotoOS is NOT a party to that service contract.
- **No Liability for Service Failures**: PhotoOS is not responsible or liable if a Photographer cancels, delivers late, produces poor quality images, or fails to perform. Photographers are solely responsible for their craft, deliverables, equipment, and statutory tax compliance.
- **No Liability for Payment Defaults**: PhotoOS is not responsible or liable if a Client defaults, delays payment, initiates an unjustified bank chargeback, or commits fraud against a Photographer.
- **Payment Facilitation**: Payments are processed through RBI-authorized payment aggregators. PhotoOS acts strictly as a commercial payment-facilitating intermediary and is not a bank, NBFC, or escrow guarantor.

## 2. Independence of the Service Contract & Disclaimer of Agency
2.1. **Direct Photographer-Client Relationship:** The agreement for photography coverage, creative direction, on-site attendance, image editing, and final physical/digital deliverables is a **direct bilateral contract solely between the Client and the Photographer**.

2.2. **No Partnership or Employment:** Nothing in this Agreement creates any partnership, joint venture, employer-employee, agency, or franchise relationship between PhotoOS and any Photographer or Client. Photographers are independent third-party service providers.

2.3. **Absence of Platform Guarantees:** PhotoOS does NOT endorse, vet the artistic talent of, guarantee the character or background of, or insure any Photographer or Client. PhotoOS does not guarantee that any booking will be completed, that photos will meet subjective aesthetic standards, or that payments will be realized without dispute.

## 4. Photographer & Studio Obligations
Every Photographer utilizing PhotoOS covenants, represents, and warrants that:
4.1. **Truth in Listing & Portfolio:** All portfolio photographs, videos, price lists, package inclusions, and equipment lists displayed on their PhotoOS profile represent their own genuine creative work. Using stock imagery or another creator's photographs without credit is strictly prohibited.

4.2. **Consents and Model Releases:** Photographers are solely responsible for obtaining written model releases, parental consent for minors, and property shoot permits for all images uploaded to PhotoOS, in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

4.3. **Contractual Honor & Delivery Timelines:** Photographers must honor confirmed bookings, arrive punctually with backup professional equipment, and deliver agreed proofs, albums, and digital files within the timeline specified in the booking confirmation.

4.4. **Tax Compliance:** Photographers are solely responsible for determining, charging, collecting, and remitting all applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) and income taxes arising from their earnings.

## 5. Client Obligations
Every Client booking or interacting through PhotoOS covenants and warrants that:
5.1. **Accurate Event Specifications:** Clients must provide accurate shoot schedules, venue addresses, coverage expectations, and special requirements prior to booking confirmation.

5.2. **Timely Payments:** Clients must fulfill all booking advances, milestone payments, and agreed balance amounts punctually through the designated platform payment gateway.

5.3. **Cooperation & Safety:** Clients agree to provide Photographers with safe working conditions, reasonable venue access, and courteous treatment during event coverage.

5.4. **Respect of Scope:** Requests outside the booked package (such as extra hours, additional crew, or raw unedited footage) must be mutually agreed upon and compensated.

## 6. Financial Architecture, Platform Fees, and Payouts
6.1. **Payment Gateway Intermediary:** All booking transactions are facilitated through RBI-authorized payment aggregators (e.g., Razorpay, Cashfree). PhotoOS acts solely as a payment-facilitating technological intermediary and **is not a bank, escrow agent, or financial guarantor**.

6.2. **Platform Commission / Technology Fee:** PhotoOS charges a platform service fee or commission (as displayed at the time of transaction) for technology hosting, lead routing, and QR gallery delivery infrastructure.

6.3. **Photographer Payouts & Holdbacks:**
- Payouts are remitted to the verified Indian bank account provided by the Photographer following successful completion of shoot milestones or gallery delivery, minus platform commissions and gateway processing charges.
- PhotoOS reserves the right to hold back funds in the event of an active dispute, substantiated non-delivery complaint, or fraud investigation until resolved.

6.4. **Gateway Surcharges & Chargebacks:** If a Client initiates an unjustified payment chargeback, the acquiring bank may deduct the disputed sum along with bank penalty fees. The Photographer agrees that PhotoOS may offset such chargebacks against future payouts if the Photographer was at fault.

## 7. Copyright, Ownership, and Licensing of Photography Deliverables
7.1. **Photographer Copyright:** Under the Indian Copyright Act, 1957, the Photographer or Studio retains the copyright in all original photographic works, raw files, and artistic negatives, unless a written copyright assignment is executed directly between the Photographer and the Client.

7.2. **Client License:** Upon full settlement of booking fees, the Client receives a non-exclusive, perpetual, personal license to download, print, display, and share delivered photographs for personal, non-commercial purposes (unless a commercial usage license is expressly agreed in writing).

7.3. **PhotoOS Technical Operating License:** Both Photographers and Clients grant PhotoOS a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free license solely to host, cache, compress, generate previews, watermarks, QR codes, and generate face-match vectors (where consented) to perform platform operations.

## 8. Cancellations, No-Shows, Rescheduling, and Refunds
8.1. **Photographer Cancellation Policies:** Photographers may select standard platform cancellation tiers (Flexible, Moderate, Strict) which govern advance deposit refunds.

8.2. **Client Cancellation:** If a Client cancels a booking, any refundable portion will be determined by the Photographer's chosen cancellation tier. Platform technology service fees already consumed are non-refundable.

8.3. **Photographer Cancellation or No-Show:** If a Photographer cancels a confirmed booking without an accepted force majeure justification:
- The Client is entitled to an immediate 100% refund of all amounts paid for that booking.
- PhotoOS may impose penalty points, demote marketplace ranking, or suspend the Photographer's account for repeated unexcused cancellations.

8.4. **Rescheduling:** Rescheduling of shoot dates is subject to mutual written agreement between the Client and Photographer, subject to photographer availability.

## 9. Prohibited Conduct and Off-Platform Dealing

9.2. **Penalties for Circumvention:** PhotoOS reserves the right to immediately suspend accounts and claim liquidated damages equal to the platform fee that would have been earned on circumvented bookings.

9.3. **Unlawful Content & Harassment:** Harassment, threats, hate speech, upload of non-consensual imagery, or fraudulent reviews will result in permanent blacklisting and reporting to law enforcement authorities.
